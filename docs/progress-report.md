# Visa Selfie progress report

October 1, 2026

Visa Selfie now has an online demonstration available at [visaselfie.duckdns.org](https://visaselfie.duckdns.org/login), with a secure connection. The platform brings applicant registration, video collection and staff review together in one place.

The administration area allows staff to create applicant records and follow each person’s progress, from registration to video submission and review. Each applicant receives a private registration link, valid for 48 hours, which staff can copy and send through WhatsApp. Replacement links can also be issued when needed.

Applicants can enter their personal details, read and accept the consent notice, and record an 18-second video by following on-screen movement instructions. They can watch the recording and retake it before submitting. The platform confirms successful submissions and provides guidance when a link has expired or a recording needs to be repeated.

Video checks have been added to assess lighting, sharpness, face positioning, the presence of a single face and completion of the requested movements. These checks provide feedback on recording quality and support staff review; they do not certify identity or liveness.

Authorized staff can watch and download submitted videos, mark them as reviewed or delete them. Registration details and consent records remain available after a video is deleted, and access to recordings is restricted to authorized administrators.

Completed tests have covered registration, consent, link expiration, rejection of unsuitable recordings and administrator access. Backup and recovery procedures have also been tested successfully.
